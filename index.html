01 / THE QUESTION

An average can hide
the decisions that changed.

A contract and its target hypothesis stay fixed. Yet showing other hypotheses, requesting more labels, or changing output order can change the answer.

Corrections and regressions can cancel in aggregate accuracy. And a model that always agrees with itself can still be consistently wrong.

THE STRICTER QUESTION

Is the target correct in all twelve responses?

Agreement alone is insufficient. We separately count consistently correct targets, stable errors, changing valid labels, and targets with an invalid response.

Official ContractNLI test split; classification only. The baseline jointly requests all 17 labels once per contract. The 30-target panel is a subset of the baseline contracts. Extra outputs in C/D do not add primary targets. For Jev, C/D changes question-key order, which differs from autoregressive output positioning.

03 / THE EVIDENCE

A trade-off,
not a single winner.

Jev has the lowest recorded cost and median response time in these configurations. Hosted language models achieve higher baseline accuracy. Persistent correctness tells a different story.

Higher accuracy has a price.

Flash-Lite gains 5.16 percentage points over Jev at about 6× its baseline cost. Gemini Pro has the highest observed accuracy, at 83.21%.

02

The ranking can change.

Sonnet has 24 of 30 targets correct in all twelve responses; Jev has 23. Small gaps on this panel do not establish a general stability advantage.

03

Consistency can be wrong.

Jev produces the same wrong label in all twelve responses for 5 of 30 targets. Stable errors are reported separately from persistent correctness.

How to read these results

Cost is configuration-specific. API costs use reported charges or recorded token rates. Local estimates value observed GPU time at $2.00/GPU-hour, excluding loading, idle allocation, storage, transfer, and taxes.

Time includes the deployment. Median client elapsed time includes preparation, network, service processing, and validation. It does not isolate model computation or time to first token.

Settings are unequal. Reasoning effort, generation limits, interfaces, hardware, and providers differ. Sonnet, Haiku, and Terra were added after inspecting earlier results.

The sample bounds the claim. The repeated panel has 30 independent contracts, not 360. Intervals use 5,000 whole-contract bootstrap draws and are descriptive, without multiple-comparison adjustment.

4,830 recorded attempts · 13 invalid responses. Failures count as incorrect and remain in cost and response-time summaries. No failed attempt was repaired, replaced, or retried.
